At Anchal Industries, we believe that high-quality fabrication is only complete with professional, precise onsite installation. Our specialized engineering crew travels directly to your residential, commercial, or farm site to handle heavy structural erection, compound gate alignment, staircase anchoring, and rolling shutter fitments.

We utilize advanced laser alignment levels, high-power mobile welding generators, and heavy-duty masonry anchors to ensure your fabricated works are permanently and safely secured. We guarantee absolute stability and compliance with civil building standards.
