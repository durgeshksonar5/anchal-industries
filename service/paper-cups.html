Anchal Industries is a proud diversified manufacturer of eco-friendly, premium-quality hygienic paper cups. Serving cafes, offices, hotels, and wholesalers across Maharashtra, our automatic manufacturing division produces biodegradable paper cups utilizing food-grade PE-coated board material and non-toxic food-safe inks.

Our automatic machines run at high speeds under strict sanitary environments to manufacture paper cups in multiple popular sizes (including 65ml, 80ml, 110ml, and 150ml). The cups are highly leak-proof, sturdy, and designed to withstand hot tea, coffee, or cold beverages.

Hygienic, leak-proof paper packaging

We provide paper cups in bulk volumes with premium rigid bottoms and tightly rolled lips, ensuring they never leak or deform when held.
