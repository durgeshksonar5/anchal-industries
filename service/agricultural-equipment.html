Anchal Industries is a trusted leader in manufacturing high-performance, robust agricultural equipment and tools. Designed to withstand demanding farming conditions, our implements—including heavy-duty tractor trolleys, precision cultivators, seed drills, and plows—are built from premium-grade structural steel and engineered for absolute reliability.

Our machinery is custom-tailored to suit diverse soil types and farming scales in Maharashtra. We incorporate high-tolerance wheel shafts, reinforced chassis frames, and high-tensile fasteners to guarantee years of hassle-free agricultural operation.

Engineered for modern agriculture

We combine traditional field-tested designs with modern fabrication tolerances. This ensures that every cultivator, seed drill, and trolley offers maximum structural stability and increases field efficiency.

01

Chassis structural welding

We weld reinforced high-gauge channels to build load-bearing chassis frames that never sag or bend under load.

02

High-tolerance tooling

Tines, blades, and seed dispensers are cut and aligned precisely to ensure consistent soil penetration and seeding.

03

Heavy-duty finishing

Applying thick layers of weather-resistant industrial glossy polyurethane paint to protect implements from moisture and soil acids.

Farming implements built to last

Reinforced structural steel

High-grade wheel hubs & axles

Flexible custom sizing

Seamless Assembly
