Anchal Industries is a premium manufacturer of high-security industrial, commercial, and residential rolling shutters. Built to protect warehouses, shops, garages, and factories, our shutters are constructed from heavy-gauge galvanized steel slats, engineered for absolute structural integrity and silent, reliable operation.

We provide manually operated pull-down shutters, gear-operated heavy shutters, and advanced motorized shutters equipped with reliable high-torque motors and remote controls. Our shutters ensure seamless safety and high wind load protection.

Engineered secure warehouse barriers

We provide custom onsite measurements and design shutters with specific gauge thicknesses (from 18G to 22G) depending on your warehouse size, security levels, and span requirements.

Precision slat roll-forming

We manufacture highly uniform interlinked galvanized steel slats on custom roll-forming machines.

Torsion spring calibration

Springs are grease-packed and balanced perfectly inside high-strength pipe shafts for effortless lifting.

03

Onsite guide track assembly

Installing thick, deep side channels and secure locking brackets for absolute wind and burglar resistance.
